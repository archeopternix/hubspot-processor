Summary thyssenkrupp Uhde Engineering Services GmbH is a Dortmund-registered German GmbH and wholly owned thyssenkrupp subsidiary providing engineering, procurement, construction, commissioning, maintenance, and plant-optimization services for industrial process facilities.  Organizational structure - Legal form: Gesellschaft mit beschränkter Haftung (GmbH). - Registered office: Friedrich-Uhde-Straße 15, 44141 Dortmund, Germany. - Part of the thyssenkrupp Uhde organization and included as a wholly owned company in thyssenkrupp AG’s equity-interest reporting.  Services and capabilities - Plant and process engineering. - Steel and piping construction. - Refractory engineering and refractory installation. - Electrical, instrumentation, measurement, and control engineering and installation. - Procurement support, construction and erection supervision, commissioning, maintenance, repair, and turnaround support. - Revamps, modernization, debottlenecking, optimization, FEED, basic engineering, detail engineering, process automation, and project management for existing plants. - Manufacture and repair of industrial equipment, including work performed at refinery and technical-center facilities.  Customer groups and markets - Chemical, petrochemical, refining, food-industry, coke-plant, and other process-industry operators. - Customers and customer groups identified in company materials include Shell, LyondellBasell, Stepan, Vestolit, Vinnolit, and Lanxess. - Projects are delivered internationally, including engineering, site, commissioning, and lifecycle-support work.  Business strategy and differentiators - Focuses on extending the operating life, availability, capacity, efficiency, and environmental performance of existing industrial plants. - Offers services across the plant lifecycle, from feasibility studies and engineering through procurement, construction, commissioning, operation support, maintenance, and modernization. - Differentiates through integration with the thyssenkrupp Uhde technology and project network, process-engineering expertise, and services aimed at debottlenecking, process improvement, and reduction of CO2 footprints.  History and major milestones - The company’s articles of association date to December 12, 1969, and the business has historical links to August Klönne. - Historical records identify previous legal names including Uhde Services GmbH and STILL OTTO MONTAGE GMBH. - The current legal entity, thyssenkrupp Uhde Engineering Services GmbH, was entered in the Dortmund Commercial Register on August 4, 2020, following a legal-form transformation, a name change, and relocation of the registered office from Haltern am See to Dortmund. - thyssenkrupp AG’s published 2024/2025 equity-interest information lists the company as a 100%-owned subsidiary.
